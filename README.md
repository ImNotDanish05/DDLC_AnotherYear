# Doki Doki Literature Club: Another Year, Another Page

A custom Doki Doki Literature Club fan mod created by **ImNotDanish05**.

---

## 📖 About the Game

*"When you're ten, turning twenty-one sounds like you'll be driving a sports car, wearing a sharp coat, and casually knowing how mortgages work. When you actually hit twenty-one, you just wake up with a stiff neck, dry eyes, and the lingering dread that you forgot to commit a branch before passing out at 3:45 AM."*

**Another Year, Another Page** follows **Danish (MC)** on his 21st birthday. 

Balancing his fifth semester of college, a demanding 20-hour-a-week software engineering internship with a boss pushing him to lock in a contract through graduation, and an AI/tech industry that reinvents itself every few weeks (from n8n to OpenClaw to Hermes), Danish is quietly suffocating under burnout and career anxiety. Between hacking together custom Minecraft plugins to remember why he loved coding in the first place and building a secret platform named "KUVART" with his circle of friends, Danish feels like the ground beneath him won't stop shaking.

Through grounded, daily conversations and heartfelt moments with the Literature Club—Sayori, Natsuki, Yuri, and Monika—Danish learns that growing up doesn't mean having your whole entire future solved before the sun goes down, and that no matter how terrifying the future feels, he isn't walking the tracks alone.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Grounded, Natural Dialogue**: Casual, daily English banter inspired by *UNBEATABLE*, *Night in the Woods*, *OMORI*, and classic *DDLC*.
* **Authentic Relatability**: A personal story exploring real-world tech burnout, college fatigue, existential dread, and deep childhood bonds.
* **5-Chapter Narrative Arc**:
  * **Chapter 1**: *The Quiet Morning* (Birthday dread, Minecraft bugs, Sayori walk & heartfelt reassurance)
  * **Chapter 2**: *Baking, Code, and Custom Cakes* (Natsuki 1-on-1, baking vs code metaphor, custom Minecraft cake)
  * **Chapter 3**: *A Cup of Roasted Barley* (Yuri 1-on-1, 1884 book on human depth vs machine speed)
  * **Chapter 4**: *Twenty-One Candles* (Full club surprise party, KUVART boys group call chaos, Monika's piano & speech)
  * **Chapter 5**: *Streetlights & Rainclouds* (Night walk home with Sayori, addressing rainclouds & future fears, finding peace)

## 🎮 How to Install & Play

This mod runs directly on the original Doki Doki Literature Club game engine (compatible with both Steam and standalone versions).

1. Download a fresh copy of **Doki Doki Literature Club** from [ddlc.moe](https://ddlc.moe/) or Steam.
2. Download or clone this repository.
3. Copy the contents of the `game/` folder from this repository into the `game/` directory of your vanilla DDLC installation (overwrite files if prompted).
4. Launch the game using `DDLC.exe` (Windows) or `./DDLC.sh` (Linux).
